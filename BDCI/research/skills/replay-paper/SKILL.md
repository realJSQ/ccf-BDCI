# Evidence-bound replay paper

Write, review and revise an English ICLR-format development-study manuscript
from independently replayed saved evidence. No new scientific experiment runs.
The review binds to the exact current draft and evidence hashes; quotations
ground issues but do not certify their reasoning or their resolution.

Campaign replay-paper-v1: exactly three planned model requests, maximum three,
5000 output tokens per request, stop future calls after 60000 returned tokens.
These are writing requests, separate from exhausted research-v2. No retries or
ledger reset. Default offline; saved raw responses can be resumed locally.
