# Method revision

Use the native SwarmFlow workflow to propose, challenge and refine a research
question after a failed exploratory pilot. Inputs include explicitly curated
primary-source reading notes and the actual earlier failure. The agent chooses
the question; developer suggestions are nonbinding implementation capabilities.

The workflow makes at most three sequential role calls. Each response is saved
before strict local validation. It performs no experiments and executes no
model-generated code. A refined plan is a development handoff, not a final
topic, a verified novelty claim or a successful experiment. Offline responses
are explicit contract fixtures. Follow-up search queries remain an outstanding
obligation before a scientific claim is frozen.
