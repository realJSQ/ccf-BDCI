# Publication manuscript revision

Use the native writer, internal reviewer, and reviser workflow with `run_publication_revision.py`. First prepare and inspect the complete writer prompt and the dependent-role templates; `--live` is a separate explicit action. Do not resend an attempted run. Saved responses can be revalidated and compiled with zero-API `--resume`.

Preserve experimental evidence and source versions, attach citations to claims, and write for scientific readers. Old submitted PDFs and external-review tokens remain bound to those old bytes. A revised PDF requires its own external review.
