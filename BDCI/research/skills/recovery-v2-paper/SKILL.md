# Recovery-v2 evidence-bound manuscript

Write, internally review and revise an English ICLR manuscript from verified
recovery_v2_evidence/1 inputs and retrieved source notes. This skill consumes
saved results and does not run new scientific experiments. The renderer owns
the scenario/policy and paired-base tables. Review hashes and quotations bind
the critique to the current draft; structural validation is not scientific
certification or the official external Agentic Reviewer.

The workflow has three roles, each invoked once. Token and text length have no
artificial limit; the runner uses the provider's available output capacity and
records usage. Preserve request ledgers and unknown-request protection. Offline
responses are fixtures and must not be represented as real generated research.
